What the site provides

The site publishes letter grades and related statistics for current members of the U.S. Congress, calculated from public records, along with articles that explain them. The grades are an opinion about legislative participation, based on the method described on the methodology page. They are not an official rating, an endorsement or a statement about any member's character, competence or fitness for office.

Accuracy

The data is rebuilt every night from Voteview, Congress.gov and other public sources, and care is taken to calculate it correctly. Even so, the sources can contain errors or delays, bill progress is estimated from each bill's latest recorded action, and the site's own code can have mistakes. The site is provided "as is", without any warranty that the information is complete, accurate or current. Please check important facts against the official record, which each report card links to. If you find an error, please report it.

Using the grades, badges and images

You are welcome to:

Campaigns and organizations may cite a member's grade, but not in a way that implies the site supports or opposes anyone.
