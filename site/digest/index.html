Week ending October 24, 2026

Here is what changed in the public record of Congress from October 17 to October 24, 2026.

Votes this week

Neither chamber held a roll-call vote this week.

Bills that became law

  • HR 3620: Southcentral Foundation Land Transfer Act of 2025. Sponsored by Nicholas J. Begich III (R, AK At-large).
  • HR 837: To require the Secretary of Agriculture to convey the Pleasant Valley Ranger District Administrative Site to Gila County, Arizona.. Sponsored by Elijah Crane (R, AZ-2).
  • HR 6380: Chiricahua National Park Act. Sponsored by Juan Ciscomani (R, AZ-6).
  • HR 227: Clergy Act. Sponsored by Vince Fong (R, CA-20).
  • HR 7831: License to Drill Act. Sponsored by Mike Kennedy (R, UT-3).
  • HR 952: Reversionary Interest Conveyance Act. Sponsored by Doris O. Matsui (D, CA-7).
  • HR 1721: Critical Infrastructure Manufacturing Feasibility Act. Sponsored by Mariannette Miller-Meeks (R, IA-1).
  • HR 7022: Mystic Alerts Act. Sponsored by August Pfluger (R, TX-11).
  • HR 2388: Lower Elwha Klallam Tribe Project Lands Restoration Act. Sponsored by Emily Randall (D, WA-6).
  • HR 4467: Vicksburg National Military Park Boundary Modification Act. Sponsored by Bennie G. Thompson (D, MS-2).
  • HR 2481: Romance Scam Prevention Act. Sponsored by David G. Valadao (R, CA-22).
  • HR 4307: Enhancing Detection of Human Trafficking Act. Sponsored by Tim Walberg (R, MI-5).
