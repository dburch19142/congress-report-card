Report a mistake

If a grade or a number looks wrong, please send:

  • the member's name,
  • which number is wrong, and
  • a link to the public record that shows the right one, if you have it.

The numbers come straight from Voteview and Congress.gov, so an error there will show up here until the source is fixed. You can also open an issue on GitHub.

Disagree with a grade?

The methodology page explains exactly how grades are calculated. The same rules are applied to every member, and grades are not changed on request. Suggestions for improving the method are welcome.

Contact your member of Congress

This site can't pass messages to Congress. To reach your own representative or senators, use the official website linked on their report card page.
